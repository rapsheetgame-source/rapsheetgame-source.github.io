# Rap Sheet — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 27, 2026
**Developer:** LLgengames
**Contact:** rapsheetgame@gmail.com

Rap Sheet is a single-player game. We built it to work without collecting your personal information. This policy explains what data the game uses and where it goes.

## What the game stores

Your saved lives, your stats, your achievements and your settings are stored **only on your device**. We don't have a server, and this data is never sent to us.

## What the game does not do

- No account or sign-up is required.
- No advertising.
- No analytics or tracking.
- We don't sell or share any data with anyone.

## Bug reports (optional)

If you choose to tap **Report a Bug** in Settings, the game opens your email app with a message addressed to us. Before you send it, you can read and change everything in it. The message includes:

- the game version, your device model and operating system,
- details about your current life in the game (age, week, mode, city), the last few events, and any errors the game ran into,
- whatever you write.

If you send it, we receive the email and your email address. We use them only to look into and fix the problem, and to reply to you if needed. We don't use them for marketing. Email us to ask for your bug reports to be deleted.

## Purchases

If the game offers in-app purchases, they are handled entirely by **Google Play** or the **Apple App Store**. We never see your payment details. The store tells the game what you bought, so the game can unlock it and restore it on a new device. See Google's and Apple's privacy policies for how they handle your information.

## Achievements

If you use **Google Play Games** (Android) or **Game Center** (iPhone), the game sends your achievements to that service. Your profile on those services is managed by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies. We don't receive your personal information from them.

## Children

Rap Sheet is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information through a bug report, email us and we'll delete it.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored on your device. For bug-report emails, contact us at rapsheetgame@gmail.com.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above.

## Contact

Questions? Email **rapsheetgame@gmail.com**.
