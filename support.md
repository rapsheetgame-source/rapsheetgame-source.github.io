# Rap Sheet — Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? We'd love to hear from you.

**Email:** rapsheetgame@gmail.com

## Reporting a bug

The fastest way is in the game: **Settings → Help → Report a Bug**. It opens your email app with the details we need already filled in. Just add a line about what happened and send it.

## Common questions

**Where are my saved lives?**
They're stored on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

**I bought something. How do I get it back on a new phone?**
Sign in to the same Google Play or Apple account, open the game, and use **Restore Purchases**.

**Is Rap Sheet for kids?**
No. Rap Sheet is a crime life sim for teens and adults. See the age rating on the store.
