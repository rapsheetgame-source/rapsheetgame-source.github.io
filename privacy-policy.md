# Rap Sheet — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 1, 2026
**Developer:** LLgengames
**Contact:** rapsheetgame@gmail.com

Rap Sheet is a single-player game. The game itself doesn't collect your personal information, and we don't have a server. The free version shows ads, and the ad service collects some information from your device to show them. This policy explains what data the game uses and where it goes.

## What the game stores

Your saved lives, your stats, your achievements and your settings are stored **only on your device**. They are never sent to us.

## Advertising (free version)

The free version of Rap Sheet shows ads from **Google AdMob**. Buying the full game removes the ads.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob (Google) collects information from your device, including:

- your device's **advertising ID** and other device identifiers,
- your **approximate location** (based on your IP address),
- how you interact with ads (views and taps),
- crash and performance information.

This information goes to Google, not to us. Google uses it under its own privacy policy: see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Your choices:**

- **Personalized ads:** where the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings.
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in your phone's settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).
- **No ads at all:** the full game has no ads.

## What we don't do

- No account or sign-up is required.
- No analytics or tracking of our own.
- We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone except as described in this policy (ads served by Google AdMob in the free version).

## Bug reports (optional)

If you choose to tap **Report a Bug** in Settings, the game opens your email app with a message addressed to us. Before you send it, you can read and change everything in it. The message includes:

- the game version, your device model and operating system,
- details about your current life in the game (age, week, mode, city), the last few events, and any errors the game ran into,
- whatever you write.

If you send it, we receive the email and your email address. We use them only to look into and fix the problem, and to reply to you if needed. We don't use them for marketing. Email us to ask for your bug reports to be deleted.

## Purchases

The full game is a one-time purchase, with optional supporter prices that unlock the same full game. Purchases are handled entirely by **Google Play** or the **Apple App Store**. We never see your payment details. The store tells the game what you bought, so the game can unlock it and restore it on a new device. See Google's and Apple's privacy policies for how they handle your information.

## Achievements

If you use **Google Play Games** (Android) or **Game Center** (iPhone), the game sends your achievements to that service. Your profile on those services is managed by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies. We don't receive your personal information from them.

## Age

Rap Sheet is intended for adults (18 and older). It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information through a bug report, email us and we'll delete it.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored on your device. For bug-report emails, contact us at rapsheetgame@gmail.com. For data collected by Google AdMob, use your phone's ad settings (above) or Google's privacy tools.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above.

## Contact

Questions? Email **rapsheetgame@gmail.com**.
