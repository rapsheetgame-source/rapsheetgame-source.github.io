# Rap Sheet — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 10, 2026
**Developer:** LLgengames
**Contact:** rapsheetgame@gmail.com

Rap Sheet is a single-player game. We don't have a server, and you don't need an account. The game uses a few outside services that collect some information from your device: **Unity Analytics** (how the game is used, so we can improve it), **Google AdMob** (ads in the free version) and the **app store** (purchases). This policy explains what data the game uses and where it goes.

## What the game stores

Your saved lives, your stats, your achievements and your settings are stored **only on your device**. They are never sent to us.

## Analytics (Unity)

The game is built with the Unity engine and uses **Unity Analytics** to help us understand how the game is played and where it has problems. Unity collects information such as:

- a random identifier for your installation of the game (not your name or email),
- your device model, operating system, language and screen size,
- your **approximate location** (country or region, based on your IP address),
- when and for how long you play, and the game version,
- purchases made in the game (what was bought and the price), and crash and performance information.

This information goes to Unity Technologies, which processes it for us and under its own privacy policy: see the [Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy). We only see it as combined statistics (for example, how many people played this week). We use it to fix bugs, balance the game and decide what to improve, never to identify you, and we don't use it for advertising.

## Advertising (free version)

The free version of Rap Sheet shows ads from **Google AdMob**. Buying the full game removes the ads.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob (Google) collects information from your device, including:

- your device's **advertising ID** and other device identifiers,
- your **approximate location** (based on your IP address),
- how you interact with ads (views and taps),
- crash and performance information.

This information goes to Google, not to us. Google uses it under its own privacy policy: see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Your choices:**

- **Personalized ads:** where the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings.
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in your phone's settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).
- **No ads at all:** the full game has no ads.

## What we don't do

- No account or sign-up is required.
- We don't run any servers or tracking of our own, and we never see your name, email or contacts through the game.
- We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone except the services described in this policy (Unity Analytics, Google AdMob in the free version, and the app store for purchases).

## Bug reports (optional)

If you choose to tap **Report a Bug** in Settings, the game opens your email app with a message addressed to us. Before you send it, you can read and change everything in it. The message includes:

- the game version, your device model and operating system,
- details about your current life in the game (age, week, mode, city), the last few events, and any errors the game ran into,
- whatever you write.

If you send it, we receive the email and your email address. We use them only to look into and fix the problem, and to reply to you if needed. We don't use them for marketing. Email us to ask for your bug reports to be deleted.

## Purchases

The full game is a one-time purchase, with optional supporter prices that unlock the same full game. Purchases are handled entirely by **Google Play** or the **Apple App Store**. We never see your payment details. The store tells the game what you bought, so the game can unlock it and restore it on a new device. Unity Analytics records that a purchase happened and its price (see above), never your payment details. See Google's and Apple's privacy policies for how they handle your information.

## Achievements

If you use **Google Play Games** (Android) or **Game Center** (iPhone), the game sends your achievements to that service. Your profile on those services is managed by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies. We don't receive your personal information from them.

## Age

Rap Sheet is intended for adults (18 and older). It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information through a bug report, email us and we'll delete it.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored on your device. For bug-report emails, contact us at rapsheetgame@gmail.com. For data collected by Unity Analytics, you can ask Unity to access or delete it through the Unity privacy policy linked above, or email us and we'll help. For data collected by Google AdMob, use your phone's ad settings (above) or Google's privacy tools.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above.

Questions? Email **rapsheetgame@gmail.com**.
